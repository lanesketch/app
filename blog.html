Version 1.2.1: community sharing, better curves, and a long list of fixes

Posted: draft · changes since 1.2.0

1.2.1 adds a community for sharing sketches, rebuilds how curves and radii work, and fixes a lot along the way.

Community

  • Sign in with GitHub and share a sketch from the Share menu. You can update or delete your own at any time.
  • A community map (satellite by default) shows shared sketches as pins, then bounding boxes, then the drawings themselves as you zoom in. Where sketches overlap, you pick which one to view.
  • Vote on sketches, leave comments, and add a short profile linked to your GitHub.

Curves and drawing

  • Curve mode now makes a few clean radius nodes instead of many small points, and there is a new PC + PI + PT mode for every way type.
  • Every bend on a selected road, path or rail has a radius you can click and edit, and every draw mode has a radius box.
  • Changing the lane count keeps the radius. Splitting in the middle of a bend keeps the curve exactly.
  • Handles show on hover, direction arrows follow curves, and added lanes stay parallel instead of twisting.
  • Drawing modes now work when extending an existing road. The draw mode box moved to the bottom right of the map and can be dragged.

Rails

  • Rails use the same controls as roads: a lane-style edge handle to add or remove tracks, a plus at each track's end, and an arrow that extends a whole multi-track group together.

Islands

  • Right-click inside an area bounded by roads to add an island (concrete, grass, red or painted), with an optional centre island and fitted or circular shapes. It works inside a single self-crossing road too, fits flush to the surrounding pavement, and can be edited or deleted from the menu.

Intersections, crosswalks and sidewalks

  • Crosswalks now land exactly where you right-click, and raised-crosswalk arrows and stop bars line up on curved and multilane roads.
  • Corner sidewalk curves are editable, and white edge lines now follow the pavement outline of intersections.
  • Lane-adding handles stay visible on a selected segment, and centre turn lanes always start from their own node.
  • Redraw intersection now keeps its own materials (and a roundabout keeps its island and apron choice), and parking stall lines stay paint instead of turning into curbs.
  • Fixes to how levels stack on rails and in the intersection builder.

Signs and menus

  • Signs update live while you edit them, keep their size while zooming, can be uploaded from a link, and can be hidden with a Show signs toggle. Wood posts have a softer colour.
  • Right-click menus now keep track of where you clicked, even while you move the mouse to the menu.
  • Keybind hints now show at the bottom left in every mode outside the intersection builder.
  • An updated tutorial with highlighted buttons, and an optional file name box under Save / Load.
